Photo Essay: Izzy's Visit

Latest Update: 25 JUN 2026

Captured my friend Izzy on a visit to Seattle—we shot some Kodak SO-078 B&W film in the studio, and then went for a walk with the Reto Wide & Slim loaded with some very expired Kodak Vision3 500T. I've learned that as the Vision3 movie film expires, the remjet gets harder and harder to remove. I used the more aggressive lye-based prebath as well as wiping with a squeegee to mechanically remove as much as possible, but you can see in some of these frames there's still a little bit of remjet left over.

The socks/hands double exposure concept is credit to Tina Ballew—Tina is a master of really creative and well composed studio concepts and this one is all hers.
