Photo Essay: Bay Flight 2026

Latest Update: 07 JUN 2026

Intro

These images were captured on a flight over the San Francisco Bay with friend and pilot Seb Schweers (@herrschweers). We took off from Gnoss Field, KDVO, and flew south to circle around downtown San Francisco and the Golden Gate Bridge. I first finished a roll of expired Kodak Vision3 500T and then loaded a roll of Velvia 50 that I had brought on the trip just for this flight. I also shot several photos with the Polaroid 195 on Polaroid ID-UV film, as well as a 16mm movie filmed on Ektachrome with my Canon Scoopic. The 35mm images are all from my Canon EOS 7S with the EF 50mm f/1.4 lens.
