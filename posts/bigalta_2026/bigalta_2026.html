Photo Essay: Big Alta 28k 2026

Latest Update: 07 JUN 2026

Intro

I brought my Canon EOS 7S and 50mm lens to the Big Alta 28k in California this year. I shot one roll of Kodak Tri-X and one roll of "Trauma Film" which is very expired Kodak 5247 movie film I got from Fabrice Ducouret (@fabulousrice). Most of the folks in these photos are my friends from Cowgill, but there are a few strangers as well. I've also included two frames of peel-apart Polaroid ID-UV, shot on the Polaroid 195 camera.
